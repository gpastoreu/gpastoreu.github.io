Brief

Giancarlo Pastor is a research and innovation professional with more than 15 years of experience across higher education, innovation and entrepreneurship. His career spans universities, research organisations, consulting, industry-led projects and public funding agencies, with a recurring focus on AI.

He currently works at the University of Helsinki, developing large-scale collaborative initiatives connecting research organisations and technology companies across Europe. He is also Adjunct Professor of Entrepreneurship at IE Business School, where he teaches deep-tech entrepreneurship and EU funding for technology startups.

He also advises a European Commission-funded 6G initiative on AI law and ethics and has served as evaluator, reviewer or expert for 10+ funding organisations. His academic and scientific track record includes 30+ journal articles, conference papers and technical reports, and 20+ journal and conference reviewing roles.

Expertise

01

Funding Roadmap

For SMEs and scale-ups

A multi-year public-funding roadmap linking technology development and growth milestones with European and national instruments. It maps relevant programmes, sequences opportunities over time, defines project concepts and maturity progression, identifies consortium structures, partner profiles, roles and dependencies, and aligns funding decisions with product, commercialization and scale-up milestones. The output is a funding architecture showing which instruments fit each development stage, what capabilities and partnerships are needed, when applications should be prepared and how successive projects can reinforce one another over time.

02

Pilot Design

For large enterprises

A structured pilot blueprint for testing technology against a defined business or technical challenge. It covers use-case definition, problem framing, technical and operational requirements, data requirements and availability, stakeholder roles and interfaces, partner profiles, implementation architecture, governance, KPIs, validation criteria, timelines and key risks. The output defines what is tested, which data and infrastructure are required, who is involved, how performance is evaluated and what evidence is needed to support adoption, procurement, investment, deployment and scale-up decisions across subsequent implementation stages.

03

AI Ethics Assessment

For AI projects and programmes

An independent assessment framework for ethical, legal and governance risks in AI-related research, innovation and technology programmes. It covers transparency, human oversight, fairness, accountability, privacy and data use, societal impacts, risk mitigation and alignment with European requirements and project commitments. Designed as a lifecycle control mechanism rather than a final compliance check, it identifies risks, documents their significance and defines proportionate mitigation measures. Deliverables can include ethics reviews, risk registers, governance recommendations, use-case assessments and reporting across the lifecycle.
